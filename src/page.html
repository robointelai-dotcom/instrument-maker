Instrument Maker

Play a few notes. Get an instrument that covers every key, made only from your own recording.

1Record your notes

Guided recording tells you what to play, and stops by itself after each note. The demo shows the screens with made-up notes, without the microphone.

Ready.

  • Play one note at a time and let it ring until it stops.
  • Leave two seconds of silence between notes. The silence is how the noise is learned.
  • Any notes, any order. You can record again to add more.
  • Sampling an app on this device? Screen-record it with sound, then choose that video here.
